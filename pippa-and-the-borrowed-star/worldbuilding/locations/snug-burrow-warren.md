## Description

The Snug Burrow Warren curls in warm tunnels under Honeycrust Bakery. Round doors open to round rooms. The walls smell of milk and fresh earth. Braided rugs cover the floors. Kettles sing low. Crumbs from the bakery drift down through cracks and moles sweep them into paths that glow amber in lamplight.

## History

Moles first dug here to stay warm in winter. Granny Nutmeg heard them shiver and left oven warmth and soup by the grate. They fixed her trays and shelves in return. More animal families joined and the burrows grew into a warren.

## Culture & Customs

Warren folk knock twice before they enter. They share soup on cold nights and mend blankets together. Newcomers get a warm stone for their pocket and a name song.

## Notable Features

Crumb Hall for meals and songs. Bram's Fix-It Nook full of spare springs and string. The Wool Pile for naps. The Root Pantry stocked with carrots, apples, oats and cheese.

## Current State

The warren feels cozy and busy. Lanterns are packed and soup stays hot for travelers. Bram readies his cart. All wait to help Pippa carry the light uphill.
