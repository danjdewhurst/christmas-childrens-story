## Description

Tinsel Hollow sits in a soft dip between pine woods. Snow rests on red roofs. Smoke curls from chimneys and smells of pine and fresh bread. Lamps glow warm along cobbled lanes. Bells ring slow from the tower. Mice, sparrows, moles, rabbits and people share the same streets, and small footprints mix with boot prints in the snow.

## History

Long ago travelers lost their way in a snowstorm. They saw a light in the hollow and followed it down to safety. They stayed and built small houses round the fire. Each year they hang lamps outside to thank the light that led them home.

## Culture & Customs

Folks in the Hollow greet by sharing something warm. A song or a warm bun. On Christmas Eve all lamps stay lit till morning. Children leave pinecones on doorsteps for luck and neighbors swap soup recipes.

## Notable Features

Honeycrust Bakery where Granny Nutmeg bakes. The Old Clock Tower that keeps village time. Lantern Lane where lamps stay lit all night. The frozen pond where skates squeak.

## Current State

Snow falls soft. The Christmas Star looks dim and the village feels hushed. People feel tired but stay kind. They wait for light to return before Christmas morning.
