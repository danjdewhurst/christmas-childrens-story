## Description

Starberry Hill rises north of the village past the pine woods. Snow lies smooth and deep. Red starberries peek through the white. At the top stands the wishing pine, tall and kind with wide branches. Ribbons, pinecones, wooden stars and dried orange slices hang from its boughs and stir in the wind. The air tastes clean and cold.

## History

Long ago a spark from the Christmas Star fell here and a pine grew where it landed. Children began to tie wishes to its branches. When a wish came true they came back to hang a wooden star to say thanks.

## Culture & Customs

Visitors walk up soft and slow. They whisper a wish and leave a berry for birds. They take only fallen sticks and leave the hill tidy.

## Notable Features

The wishing pine at the crown. Starberry bushes along the path. The Stone Nest where the Star can rest. The Sledding Slope on the south side.

## Current State

The hill waits quiet under snow. The wishing pine holds a faint glow in its bark. The Stone Nest stands empty. Friends must climb before Christmas morning to set the light back in place.
