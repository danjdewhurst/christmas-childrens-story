## Description

The Old Clock Tower stands at the middle of Tinsel Hollow. Its gray stones feel cool and its clock face glows soft yellow at night. Inside it ticks and hums. Pippa lives behind the great gears in a nook of wool and buttons. Old Tobias keeps cocoa on the workbench. The air smells of oil and warm wood.

## History

The first families built the tower so all could wake at the same time. Old Tobias learned to wind it from his grandfather. Pippa moved in one rainy autumn and Tobias left her crumbs and a thimble bed.

## Culture & Customs

The tower has small rules. Wind the gears gently and feed the roosting sparrows. Keep quiet during nap chimes.

## Notable Features

The Gear Room with slow wheels. The Bell Loft where Wrenna naps. Pippa's Nook behind the third wheel. The Winding Stair with rest landings for lookouts over the snow.

## Current State

The clock runs a little slow while the Star fades. Tobias cleans the lens and waits. Pippa feels sorry and wants to make it right.
