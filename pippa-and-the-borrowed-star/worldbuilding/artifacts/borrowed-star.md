## Description

The Borrowed Star is small, no larger than a pinecone. It glows peach gold and pulses soft like a sleepy heartbeat. Pippa keeps it in a jam jar lantern with a brass lid punched with star holes. Twine wraps the neck for carrying. When lit it smells faintly of sugar and pine.

## Function

The Star shines when it is shared and dims when it is kept close.

1. Share to shine. A kind act, a song or a story makes the glow grow warm.
2. Say thanks. Spoken thanks keeps the light steady.
3. Let it rest. It needs dark and quiet naps in its jar, wrapped in cloth.
4. Carry together. It stays bright when many paws and hands take turns.

It cannot be owned or locked away. If held selfishly it cools and fades. If touched bare too long it tickles and slips. It must rest in the Stone Nest on Starberry Hill by Christmas morning or its light will go out.

## History

The Star hung over Tinsel Hollow for a hundred winters. It lit Lantern Lane and warmed the wishing pine. One windy night Pippa chased a moth in the Bell Loft and bumped the bell rope. The Star tumbled down. Wrenna slowed its fall and Bram caught it in a dough cloth.

## Current State

The Star lives in the jam jar lantern and travels with Pippa. It flickers low but stays alive. Its warmth comes and goes with the kindness round it. Friends guard it on the climb to the hill.
