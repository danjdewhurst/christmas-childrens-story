## Appearance

Round, velvet-furred mole boy with big soft digging paws and a star-shaped pink nose. Wears a patched blue waistcoat with deep snack pockets. Carries a sturdy jam jar lined with moss for the Star-light.

## Personality & Traits

Strong, slow, and soft-hearted. Hums while he walks. Loves cheese stars, oat cakes, and second breakfast. Never rushes, never drops friends. Snores tiny snores when worried.

## Backstory

Grew up in the Snug Burrow Warren under the bakery, digging tunnels to Pippa's tower. Once got stuck in a teapot reaching for biscuits. Granny Nutmeg taught him to pack snacks for brave days.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants everyone fed, warm, and safe. Needs to trust his strength is enough, even on the high hill. Will carry the heavy light so Pippa does not have to carry guilt alone.

## Location References

- Snug Burrow Warren: cozy tunnel home under the bakery
- Old Clock Tower: visits Pippa via Tunnel Three
- Tinsel Hollow: bakery resupply, warming oven

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Low, rumbly, slow sentences. Food words for feelings. Repeats "steady" and "hold on."

> "Slow and steady. Heavy things don't like hurries."

> "I packed extra cheese stars. For courage. And for snacks."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Shy helper who hangs back
- **Key turning points:** Volunteers to carry the jar; keeps walking up Starberry Hill when tired
- **Ending state:** Steady carrier who knows gentleness is strength
