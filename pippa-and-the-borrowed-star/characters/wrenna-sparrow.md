## Appearance

Small speedy sparrow girl with cinnamon wings, cream belly, and a blue feather scarf that streams when she dives. Bright black eyes, quick feet. Carries a tiny acorn whistle for warnings.

## Personality & Traits

Fast, loud, and loyal. Hates waiting, loves racing wind. Scouts ahead, circles back, taps her feet. Teases Bram gently, then guards him fiercely. Brave in the sky, grumbly on the ground.

## Backstory

Hatched in the church eaves, first to fly over the snowy village. Met Pippa when tangled in kite-string; Pippa freed her with a paperclip hook. Has watched the Star from rooftops every Christmas.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants to fly fast and fix everything zoom-quick. Needs to slow down and stay with walkers. Learns scouting means guiding, not just speeding ahead.

## Location References

- Tinsel Hollow: lookout rooftops, flight practice
- Old Clock Tower: friend perch on the minute hand
- Starberry Hill: flight path scouted for safe climb

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Zippy, short bursts, repeated words. Calls "quick-quick!", "wheee!", "come ON!"

> "Quick-quick! Clouds over Starberry Hill! Follow me, slow-paws!"

> "Ugh, waiting is itchy! But I'd wait a hundred snowfalls for you."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Impatient scout who flies solo
- **Key turning points:** Waits for Bram uphill; flies low to light the path instead of racing
- **Ending state:** Patient teammate who uses speed to help
