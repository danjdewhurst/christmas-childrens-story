## Appearance

Plump hedgehog granny with cinnamon-spike fur, flour-dusted apron, and rose spectacles on a string. Tiny oven mitts, warm paws. Kitchen smells of nutmeg, honey, and fresh buns.

## Personality & Traits

Warm, bustling, and wise. Fusses, feeds, and listens well. Calls everyone "my dear." Gives courage buns for scary paths. Knows when to hug and when to push out the door.

## Backstory

Baked in the village square for forty years. Raised three hedgehoglets, now grown. Took in cold strays every winter. Her bakery became home base for Pippa's crew after the teapot incident.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants all little ones full and home by morning. Needs to send them out into the snow anyway. Offers food, wraps, and belief that small hearts carry great light.

## Location References

- Tinsel Hollow: home base bakery, oven warmth, planning table
- Snug Burrow Warren: tunnels below bakery, soup nights

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Cozy, round, food metaphors. Lots of "oh my dears", "there now", and "take two."

> "Come in, my dears! Cold paws go by the oven."

> "Courage isn't big, love. It's warm. Like a bun. Take two."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Sheltering baker who keeps children inside
- **Key turning points:** Packs food for the hill climb; keeps bakery light on all night
- **Ending state:** Home that launches bravery and welcomes return
