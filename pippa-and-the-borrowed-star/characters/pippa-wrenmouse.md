## Appearance

Tiny grey mouse girl, no taller than a teacup. Big round ears, pink nose, and a long tail that often wears a brass gear like a bracelet. Red thread-scarf, bottle-cap tool belt full of string, springs, and crumbs.

## Personality & Traits

Curious, chatty, and always building. Drops things, trips over her own tail, and hates waiting. Afraid of heights and squeaks on ladders. Kind first, brave second, sorry always ready.

## Backstory

Raised in the gears of the Old Clock Tower, lulled by ticking. Old Tobias leaves her cheese crumbs and tells star stories. She learned to tinker by fixing broken toys mice left behind.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants to fix her mistake herself and carry the Star back alone. Needs to learn to ask for help and share its light. Her fear of heights fights her promise to make it right.

## Location References

- Old Clock Tower: home nest in the chime-loft, workbench under Gear Three
- Tinsel Hollow: village lanes she runs through
- Starberry Hill: where the Star must be returned by morning

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Quick, bright, full of questions and whirring sounds. Says "ooh!", "what if", and "oops-sorry!" a lot.

> "Ooh! What if we tied the light to my kite-string. Oops, sorry!"

> "I knocked it down. So I'll carry it back. Even up high. Even if my knees wobble."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Clumsy, secretive tinkerer who hides mistakes
- **Key turning points:** Admits she knocked the Star; lets Bram carry and Wrenna lead; gives away some light to help others
- **Ending state:** Responsible sharer who knows brave means together
