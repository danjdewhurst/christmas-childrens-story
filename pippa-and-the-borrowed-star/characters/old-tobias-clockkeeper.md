## Appearance

Tall, stooped old human with silver whisker-beard, patched brown coat, and big warm hands. Brass key ring jingles at his belt. Pocket full of cheese crumbs for mice. Smells of oil and peppermint.

## Personality & Traits

Kind, slow, and notice-everything. Talks to clocks like friends. Never scolds, only mends. Remembers old star songs but forgets where he left his hat. Calm in storms.

## Backstory

Kept the village clock for fifty years. As a boy, saw the Christmas Star flicker and heard from his gran that borrowed light must be shared to stay bright. Leaves crumbs for tower mice ever since.

## Motivations & Goals

Wants the clock and village kept safe and on time. Needs to let small friends do big work without fixing it for them. Gives lore and a lantern, then trusts Pippa.

## Location References

- Old Clock Tower: workroom, gears, lore wall
- Tinsel Hollow: winds the town clock, tells stories

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Warm, slow, low. Short proverbs, gentle questions. Calls mice "little one."

> "Stars borrow their light, Pippa. So must we."

> "Wind the clock gently, little one. Tired gears need kindness too."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Watchful keeper who quietly tidies mistakes
- **Key turning points:** Tells Pippa the sharing-light lore; steps back at the hill door
- **Ending state:** Guide who trusts the young to carry on
