## Setup

Pippa lives happily low in the warm clock-tower. She loves to polish and help, but never climbs to the bell-top — heights make her tummy flutter. She also hates asking for help; she wants to fix her own mistakes alone. When she knocks the star down, she wants to carry it all by herself.

## Rising Action

1. **Too-heavy jar:** Pippa tries to carry the jar alone. It is heavy and tippy. She learns to let Bram carry, Wrenna scout, and Tobias guide.
2. **Asking in the village:** Pippa is shy to knock on doors in the dark. She practices asking kindly for passage and candle-light, and villagers happily help.
3. **Wobbly knees on the hill:** On Starberry Hill, Pippa slips and wants to stop. Bram shares his coat-cape, Wrenna shows small safe steps, Tobias reminds her to breathe and hum.

## Climax

At the Wishing Pine, only small paws can climb to the star-nest at the very top. Pippa must face her height fear. She asks her friends to hold steady, sing low, and talk her up branch by branch. She climbs scared — and does it anyway.

## Resolution

Pippa is still small and heights still feel fluttery, but now she knows: brave is not not afraid. Brave is try, and let love help. She leads the wish on Christmas morning.
