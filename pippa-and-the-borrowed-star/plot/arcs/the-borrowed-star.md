## Setup

Snowy village of Tinsel Hollow on Christmas Eve. Pippa the clock-tower mouse keeps the tower gears clean and loves the Christmas Star best of all. While dusting the bell-hammer, Pippa tickles the clock, the clock sneezes — A-CHOO! — and the tower shakes. High above, the Christmas Star wobbles, slips, and tumbles softly into the snow. It does not break, but its light is fading.

Old Tobias says: a fallen star cannot fly. It must be carried back up Starberry Hill to the top of the Wishing Pine before first morning light, or it will go out.

## Rising Action

1. **A Jar for a Star:** Pippa and friends save the starlight in a strawberry-jam jar. It becomes a warm lantern, but it flickers and needs care, songs, and sharing to stay lit.
2. **Through the Dark Village:** Without the star, Tinsel Hollow grows dim and sleepy-dark. To keep going, the friends must cross the village and learn that little shared lights help the jar stay bright.
3. **The Slippery Climb:** The path up Starberry Hill is steep, slippery, and snowy-windy. The jar almost goes out. The friends must take turns carrying, sheltering, and scouting to keep the light alive till dawn.

## Climax

At the top of the Wishing Pine, in cold first-light wind, Pippa must climb high herself to place the jar among the branches so the star can catch and climb back into the sky. She is afraid of heights, but her friends hold steady and hum below.

## Resolution

Pippa sets the light down. The star sips dawn-light, stretches, twinkles, and floats back up. Morning light spills over Tinsel Hollow. The village wakes to Christmas morning, warm and bright.
