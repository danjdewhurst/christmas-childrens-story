## Setup

Tinsel Hollow runs on starlight. Lamp-posts sip it, window stars glow with it. When the Christmas Star falls, everything dims to a sleepy blue. No one is hurt, but all feel droopy and quiet.

## Rising Action

1. **One candle shared:** Granny Nutmeg lights one candle and shares the flame with neighbors. A small chain of lights begins.
2. **Windows open:** Villagers open doors, sing walking-songs, and line the path to Starberry Hill with candle-jars so the team can see.
3. **Soup and songs for climbers:** At the hill-foot, villagers give warm hazelnut soup, dry mittens, and a going-up song that later keeps the jar lit in the wind.

## Climax

While Pippa climbs the pine, the whole village below holds up their little lights and hums the same hum — lending strength without leaving home. Their shared glow helps the star remember how to shine.

## Resolution

When the star returns, village lights bloom brighter than before. Villagers decide to keep a Share-Light Supper every Christmas Eve: soup, song, and one candle passed hand to hand. Home means keeping each other warm.
