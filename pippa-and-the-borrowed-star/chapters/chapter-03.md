## Chapter Text

The snow squeaked under Pippa's boots. The village was dark around her, but not scary dark. The lamps glowed sleepy blue, like eyes half closed. Pippa held the jam jar close to her chest. Inside, the Star gave a small sleepy blink.

Bram walked beside her. His big feet went crunch, crunch. "Slow and steady," he said. His pockets jingled with snacks. Wrenna fluttered low over their heads. "Quick-quick! No ice this way!" she called. Old Tobias came at the back with his lantern stick. The Star light wobbled in the jar.

Pippa stopped at Granny Nutmeg's door. Her tummy felt fluttery. She had practiced asking all the way down the lane. "Please, can we... please, may we..." Her voice came out tiny. Bram gave her a soft nudge. Old Tobias bent low beside her. "Go on, little one," he hummed. She knocked tap, tap, tap. "Oops-sorry!" she squeaked, even before anyone answered.

The door opened with a warm creak. Light spilled out like honey. Granny Nutmeg stood there in her patchwork shawl. She had hurried home ahead with her basket. "Why, my dears!" she said. "Come in from the cold." Pippa held the jar up high. "Ooh!" Granny said softly when she saw the Star. Pippa found her big voice deep down. "Please, may we share a little flame to keep it warm?"

Granny Nutmeg lit an extra candle. "Take two," she said. She tucked a second warm bun into Bram's pocket too. Next door, the Badger twins opened their shutters. Then Mole Mama opened hers. Then old Finch opened his. They shared a flame from one to the next. Soon a string of golden lights bobbed down Tinsel Hollow. Someone started the walking-song. "Share a spark, carry it far." Pippa sang too, soft at first. Then she sang louder. Near the shared candles, the Star stretched. It glowed brighter. Pippa felt warm right down to her toes. "Thank you," she whispered. The light grew steady.

The friends stopped at the end of the lane. Starberry Hill loomed tall and white above them. Wrenna landed low on Bram's hat. "Quick-quick! Path is clear to the foot!" she peeped. A little soup pot steamed there under a crooked pine. The smell of carrots and warm bread curled into the cold air. Pippa breathed it in deep. "Ooh!" she said.
