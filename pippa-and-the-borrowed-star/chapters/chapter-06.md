## Chapter Text

For a moment nothing moved.

Then the little star stretched. It twinkled once, soft as a yawn. A thin line of pink light spilled over Starberry Hill. Morning was coming.

Pippa held her breath. The star lifted out of the jam-jar, light as milkweed down. It hovered by her cheek, warm as a thank you. Then it floated up. Up past the ribbons. Up past Wrenna circling slow to guide it. Up until it found its place in the paling sky and settled there with a happy blink.

Below, every lamp bloomed brighter, as if the village lights had taken a deep sip too. The hum rose into a soft cheer.

Pippa climbed down into waiting arms. Bram caught her with a soft oof and held the ladder-string steady till both her feet touched snow. "Slow and steady," he said. "I have you." Nutmeg wrapped her in a warm shawl and said, "my dears." Wrenna bumped her cheek with a wing and whispered, "Quick-quick! You did it!"

Pippa looked up at the sky. "I am sorry I knocked you down," she whispered. The star twinkled back, kind and rested. Tobias laid a big warm hand near her and said, "Stars borrow their light, Pippa. So must we. You shared it well."

Her knees still wobbled. But her heart felt warm and full. She had not been brave alone. She had been brave together.

By sunrise they were all back at the bakery for Christmas morning. The oven glowed. The table held barley soup with steam curling up and honey buns with melted tops. Pippa told how the star had sipped song and thanks and rest. Nutmeg nodded and said, "Then every year, my dears, we will share soup and light on this day." And just like that the Share-Light Supper was born.

Pippa lifted her cup. "I wish for warm homes and shared light," she said. "Thank you, Bram. Thank you, Wrenna. Thank you, Tobias. Thank you, Nutmeg."

Up in the tower, Tobias wound the old clock. It ticked happy and true.

That night Pippa curled sleepy-safe in her nook under Gear Three, button held loose in one paw, while her star winked softly through the window.
