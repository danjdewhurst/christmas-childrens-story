## Chapter Text

It was Christmas Eve. Dusk turned the snow pink and blue.

Pippa Wrenmouse lived behind the gears of the Old Clock Tower. She was tiny and grey, with a red thread scarf and a tool belt that jingled when she walked.

Tonight she dusted the low gears with her feather brush. Swish, swish. The air smelled of warm oil and cocoa.

Pippa never went past the third gear. It sat too high. Her tummy flipped just to look at it.

"Ooh!" she whispered. A pale moth fluttered past her nose, soft as powder.

She pattered after it. Tip tap, tip tap went her feet on the cold brass.

"Oops-sorry!" she said when her brush tickled the big spring by mistake.

The moth landed on the clock nose wheel. Pippa reached up with her brush. Tickle, tickle.

The whole tower breathed in deep.

Then it sneezed. "A-CHOO!"

The gears shivered. The chains swung. Pippa grabbed her scarf and sat down with a plop.

High above, the Borrowed Star wobbled in its nest. It was small as a pinecone, peach-gold and warm. Every night it lit Tinsel Hollow.

Now it tipped. It rolled. It fell soft as a feather down into the snow. Poof!

Pippa peeked over the low ledge. Her heart thumped fast.

Down in the village, lamp after lamp turned sleepy blue. The light yawned and grew dim.

Pippa felt sorry and soft inside. She had not meant to make such a big sneeze.

"Are you all right, Star?" she asked in a small voice.

She scampered down the tower stairs. The snow went crunch, crunch under her boots. It smelled clean and cold.

At the bottom, something glowed in a dip in the snow. A warm peach-gold glow.

Pippa gasped. Then she remembered the empty jam jar she had saved under her workbench. Old Tobias Clockkeeper had poked star holes in the lid for her, just for fun.

She ran to fetch it. Her scarf flapped behind her.

The glow pulsed soft and slow behind her, like it was calling her to come and see.
