## Chapter Text

Pippa tipped her head back and looked up. Up. Up.

The Wishing Pine rose tall over her, kind and wide. Its bark smelled like snow and pine sap. Ribbons fluttered from its lower branches, tied there long ago for wishes. Near the very top, tucked where the branches grew close, sat the Stone Nest. It was round and empty and waiting.

"Only small paws can climb that high," Old Tobias said, soft. "The branches grow too close for big hands."

Pippa's knees wobbled. She squeezed the wobbly button on her coat. Squeeze, squeeze.

"I knocked it down," she whispered. "So I'll carry it back. Even if my knees wobble."

Bram tied his ladder-string firm around the lowest branch and held it steady with both paws. "Slow and steady," he rumbled. "I have got the string. You have got the star."

Wrenna landed light on a low branch and tapped it with her foot. "Quick-quick! This way, Pippa! Paw here, then here!"

Pippa tucked the jam-jar close, where the little star rested warm in moss. She set one paw on the string. Then the bark. Then up.

Branch by branch, her friends talked her up.

"Paw left," said Bram from below.

"Good! Now right! Quick-quick, you did it!" said Wrenna from just above.

Halfway up, Pippa's tail trembled. Below her, lights began to bloom in the dark. The village had followed all the way up the hill. Lamp after lamp lifted high. Tobias held his lantern. Nutmeg held her bakery candle. Then Pippa heard a hum. It was low and sweet and rising, like soup warming.

They were humming her star song.

Pippa squeezed her button again. "Brave is asking for help," she whispered. "And I asked."

So she climbed, scared anyway. Up past the ribbons. Up past the last pinecone. Up until her nose touched the cold stone of the Stone Nest.

Pippa breathed out slow and soft. She lifted the jam-jar and set it gently in the middle of the nest. She unscrewed the lid.

Inside, the little star shivered.

It sipped the cold night air. It sipped the pine smell and the song still rising from below.

Then it shivered once more... and glowed.
