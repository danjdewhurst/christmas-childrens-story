## Chapter Text

The wind found them at the bottom of Starberry Hill. It tugged Pippa's ears and made the snow hiss. The path up was white and slippery. Pippa took one step. Slip. She took one more. Slide. "Ooh!" she said. "It is so steep." High above, the Wishing Pine swayed at the very top.

Halfway up, the Star in the jar flickered. Its light went thin and wobbly, like a candle in wind. Pippa cupped her paws around the jar. "Please stay," she whispered. The light sank lower. Her own legs felt wobbly too. "I want to go back down," she said in a small voice.

Bram knelt down fast. "Slow and steady," he said. He opened his big coat like a cape and wrapped it round Pippa and the jar. Old Tobias leaned in close. "Easy, little one," he said. He hummed their walking-song, low and soft. Wrenna swooped low. "Quick-quick! I see mouse-steps! Zig and zag!" she cried. The wind still pushed, but inside the coat-cape it was warm and still.

Pippa remembered the saved courage bun in Bram's snack pocket. Her paws shook as she broke it in half. "One for you, Bram. One for me. Courage buns," she said. She nibbled and felt a little braver. "Thank you for waiting for me," she told the Star. "Thank you for keeping me warm," she told her friends. "Oops-sorry I am so slow," she added. The Star gave a warm pink glow. Its light steadied and grew. Bram smiled. "Brave is asking when you need help," he said.

They rested in the hollow of Pippa's scarf-sling. Pippa held the jar and sang a tiny thank-you song till her ears stopped trembling. The Star rested too, tucked warm in moss and wool. Its glow held steady and kind. Then she looked up. The Wishing Pine towered over them. Its top swayed in the dark sky. Pippa's tummy flipped. She was afraid of heights. "Ooh," she gulped. "All the way up there?"
